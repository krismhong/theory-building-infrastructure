# Source bibliography

Methodological references for the argument checks and optional writing guidance. Each entry identifies the work, the material assessed, and its relationship to the project's source history.

Use the [source-to-check map](levels/SOURCES.md) for concepts and passage locators. The [source register](evidence/SOURCE-REGISTER.json) provides the individual reading notes and links. References inform the design; the checks and their implementation remain project decisions.

For a first review, choose a level from the [five-level overview](levels/README.md), then read its row in the [source-to-check map](levels/SOURCES.md). The map explains both the source concept and the limits of applying it to this prototype. Follow its links here for the work's identity and what was read. Labels such as **R013** identify bibliography entries; a check number such as **3.1** means check 1 at the paragraph level (Level 3).

The 122-work count describes the scope of the documentation. It is not a measure of checker accuracy or 122 independent tests of the prototype.

## Coverage

This edition contains **122 verified work identities**, checked on 20 October 2026. Books, article versions, cross-journal reprints, and revised guidance are counted once per underlying work family. A verified identity is separate from a reading of the full work.

Selected text means the relevant passages were read, sometimes in an accepted or submitted manuscript; it does not mean every page or the final published version was read. Excerpts, abstracts, overviews, companion guidance, and summaries support only the concepts visible in those materials. Bibliographic-only entries are further reading, with no passage-grounded check mapping.

## Source history

- **Documented attribution (2):** existing writing guidance explicitly names these works. This establishes attribution, not the edition or extent of original reading.
- **Historical credit (40):** the work was named in earlier source records; this alone does not establish original construction use.
- **Earlier supporting source (1):** assessed during earlier documentation work.
- **New supporting source (79):** assessed for this bibliography expansion; no historical-use claim.

Bookey summaries of Cook and Silvia are identified as summaries, with summary-page locators. Their underlying editions are not established by the digests; the listed book editions were verified separately. They are not substitutes for direct inspection of the books. No source PDFs, books, or transcripts are included. Bibliography development is neither model training nor a validation of checker judgments.
