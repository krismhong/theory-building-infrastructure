# Fictional source records for the development pilot

Every work below is invented, as in sample-draft.md and sample-draft-v2.md. These records define this toy source world for the pilot; they are not real research or new evidence about teams. Both coverage modes receive the same records. These summaries were added for development on 2026-10-03, after the existing sample reports, and are not held-out material.

- Avery (2014): Deadline pressure focuses attention on the current project task. The study does not measure reflection, learning, or later-project performance.
- Brandt & Cole (2017): Teams report greater task focus under deadline pressure. The study does not establish that pressure improves learning or that it causes careful review of mistakes.
- Dorsey (2019): Observational comparisons in three software firms show that teams finishing early hold longer reviews and change more practices afterward. The findings do not isolate the cause of those differences or establish that reflection always improves later performance.
- Evers (2016): Reflection can improve performance on later projects under some conditions. The study does not establish improvement for every team or project, an effect of every additional unit of slack, or a policy that teams ought to follow.
