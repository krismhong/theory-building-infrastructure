# Answering a dispute

Until now you worked alone. Every checker has now reported, and a checker at a lower level, reading the part of the draft it checked, disputes some of your flags. Answer each dispute from your own reading of the draft, as you would defend the flag to the author.

- **keep**: the flag stands as you wrote it. Say why, quoting the draft.
- **revise**: the flag stands, but what fails, its decision, its kind, or its severity needs to change. Give the revised lines.
- **withdraw**: the flag rests on a misreading, or the draft already does what the flag asks. Say what you misread. The other checker's reading is not a reason by itself; the draft's words are.

You decide, not the other checker, and the author sees your answer.

Answer every question, in this form:

```
## Q<number>
Verdict: <keep, revise, or withdraw>
Reason: <one or two sentences, quoting the draft>
Tag: <CRITICAL, ISSUE, or SUGGEST; revise only>
What fails: <revise only>
Decision: <revise only>
Kind: <fork or slip; revise only>
```
