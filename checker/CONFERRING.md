# Conferring with the levels above you

Until now you worked alone. Every checker has now reported, and the organizer has routed to you the flags that checkers at higher levels raised on the parts of the draft that contain the parts you checked. The organizer only routes; it has not judged these flags, and neither has anyone else. Judge your own flags against them, from your own reading of the draft.

For each of your flags listed below, say one of three things:

- **stands**: your flag needs its own decision from the author.
- **follows**: the author's answer to a flag above would settle yours, because yours is the same problem seen from your level. Name that one flag, and say in one line how its answer settles yours. You may also name another of your own flags.
- **withdraw**: on reflection, your flag rests on a misreading of the draft. Say what you misread.

A flag above yours is not right because it sits higher. If one misreads the part of the draft you checked, dispute it, quoting the words that show the misreading; its own checker will answer you.

Reply in this form:

```
## Your flags
- <your flag> | stands
- <your flag> | follows | <the flag whose answer settles yours> | <one line: how>
- <your flag> | withdraw | <what you misread>

## Disputes
- <a flag above yours> | <the draft's words that show the misreading>
<or "None">
```
