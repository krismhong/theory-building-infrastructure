# Optional pass: word choice

**This pass is not a logic check.** It runs after Level 5, only when the author asks for it, and every flag it writes says "word choice, not logic." Convoluted sentences are a logic problem and are caught at Level 5 (rule 5.7); this pass covers what remains on the surface.

**Read** the whole draft, or the part your task names, and any personal-layer word list your task supplies.

## Checks

**W.1 Jargon.** A term a reader outside the field would not know, used without definition, or a technical term where a plain word says the same. Decision: "Is 'X' needed here, or does 'Y' say it?"

**W.2 The author's own list.** A word or phrase the author's personal layer marks to avoid. Decision: "Your list marks 'X'. Replace it with 'Y'?"

**W.3 Inflated or vague words.** A word that claims more than the sentence supports, or says nothing a reader can check. Decision: "What does 'X' add that the plain claim does not?"

## Pass down

None.
