# The levels

| Level | Unit | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overall argument | Are claims, premises, relationships, and boundaries coherent? |
| 2 | Headings/sections | Do they deliver the argument and promises? |
| 3 | Paragraph points | Do sibling points make the parent's case? |
| 4 | Sentences | Do they support the paragraph's point? |
| 5 | Clauses/attribution | Are reports and inferential steps clear and consistent? |

Each asks upward about role, downward about support, and across about siblings under one parent. Siblings may be complementary, alternative, or conditional. Sequential order alone does not establish logical fit.

The criteria assess arguments generally. Optional genre context adds no criteria. Style, novelty, truth, and evidence sufficiency are separate; missing context can leave indeterminate findings.

Initial tasks are independent. Only author-confirmed context may be shared. Full mode covers all assigned paragraphs/clauses; gated mode uses fewer tasks with no established accuracy advantage. Conferring differs from the later separate horizontal verifier.

First-sentence skeletons are navigation/location aids, not topic-placement requirements. Regex/word-choice passes are separate.
