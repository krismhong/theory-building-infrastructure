# Sources and checks

This map connects the current **core-v2.1-2026-10-10** checks to relevant concepts and bounded readings. The criteria remain in the individual level files. A source can explain a design choice without prescribing the exact question or validating an automated decision.

The [bibliography](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) identifies each work and its source-history category. The [source register](../evidence/SOURCE-REGISTER.json) records reading scope and notes for every entry. Page numbers refer to the specified version; a PDF page, manuscript page, and published page are not interchangeable.

## Optional guidance

Theory-writing routes can be explored through [Designing Conceptual Articles: Four Approaches](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r046), [From the Editors - The Nuts and Bolts of Writing a Theory Paper: A Practical Guide to Getting Started](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r105), [Meanings of Theory: Clarifying Theory through Typification](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r084), and [On Genre: A Few More Tips to Article-Writers](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r122). These provide alternatives rather than extra core requirements.

Word-choice context includes [Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r117), [Stylish Academic Writing](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r100), and [Line by Line: How to Edit Your Own Writing](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r013). The first two were assessed through publisher material; Cook through an attributed Bookey summary. [How to Write a Lot: A Practical Guide to Productive Academic Writing](../SOURCE-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md#r087) contributes optional writing-process advice through a Bookey summary, not an argument-validity test.

## Implementation boundary

The five-level partition, isolated checkers, later horizontal verifier, routing, findings schema, and reuse receipts are project decisions. Hierarchy and configuration literature does not establish independent errors, efficiency, or reliability. Computational discourse and citation papers are methodological background: their algorithms and reported performance are not implemented or inherited merely by citing them.

This documentation update adds source grounding without changing the check criteria. The changed reference sections change the effective definition hashes; reports and receipts must retain their recorded hashes. See [ruleset compatibility](RULESET.md).
