# Theory paper guidance (optional context)

Use with `--genre genres/theory-paper.md` when chosen by the author. This identifies parts of a theory argument; it adds no logical-defect criteria.

- Identify constructs, relationships, mechanisms, and boundaries actually claimed. A challenged assumption, puzzling observation, or outside lens can organize an argument; none is required.
- Trace definitions, derivations, alternatives, and boundaries where relevant. Propositions, process models, typologies, and other forms are legitimate. No section order is prescribed.
- Identify paragraph points in their full text. Topic placement and the number of sentences expressing a point are author choices. Check inferential support and same-parent fit.
- Separate reported literature from deductions. Source records permit fidelity checks, not findings that a theory is true. Do not require new empirical data in a conceptual argument.

Descriptive, normative, and mixed arguments are legitimate; check their stated relation rather than words such as "should". Novelty, interest, contribution, and outlet fit remain author/reviewer judgments. Clean logic establishes neither theoretical truth nor publication readiness.
